(static) lkjCorrelation(dim, etaopt, sizeopt, choleskyopt) → {Array.<Array.<number>>|Array}

Draws correlation matrices from the LKJ distribution (Lewandowski, Kurowicka & Joe, 2009), with density proportional to det(R)^(eta - 1). eta = 1 is uniform over correlation matrices; larger eta concentrates around the identity. The usual prior in Bayesian models.
