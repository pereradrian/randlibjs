# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space of the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, criticise ideas and code rather than people, accept corrections
with good grace, and do not harass anyone.

Report a problem privately to randlibjs@gmail.com. Reports are handled by the maintainers, confidentially,
and may lead to a warning, the removal of content or a ban.
